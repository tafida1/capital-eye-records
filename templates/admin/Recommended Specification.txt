6. PostgreSQL Optimization

Open PostgreSQL postgresql.conf later and adjust only if needed:

max_connections = 100
shared_buffers = 256MB
effective_cache_size = 1GB
work_mem = 4MB
maintenance_work_mem = 64MB

For a small/medium clinic, the default PostgreSQL settings are usually okay at first.
